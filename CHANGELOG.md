# Changelog

## [1.2.0] - 2026-11-23

### ✨ New

- 🏦 Connect your Mercury bank account with a Mercury API token.
- 🔗 Map each Mercury account to a new Cheddar account, including its balance.
- 📥 Choose how much history to import: 30 days, 90 days, 365 days, or all history.
- 🔄 Sync new transactions automatically and manually with “Sync now”.
- ♻️ Reconnect an expired Mercury token without losing your account mapping.
- 🏷️ Imported transactions are categorized by merchant or sender name, with new categories created automatically.
- 📝 Mercury bank descriptions and notes are preserved in the transaction description.
- 🗑️ Unlink Mercury and choose whether to keep or delete the transactions already imported.

### 🔒 Privacy

- 🔐 Your Mercury API token stays on this device and is sent only to Mercury.
